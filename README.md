# NeoCharter
An educational voyage planner interactive app
NeoCharter is an interactive educational voyage-planning app built around a representative bulk carrier that addresses a focused research question: how do key operational choices—cargo size and stowage, service speed, bunkering strategy and locations, routing (including optional canal transits), and simple port/canal economics—shape voyage profitability, fuel use and operational exposure? This question matters because everyday chartering and voyage decisions translate into large direct costs (fuel, port and canal fees, OPEX) and revenues, and because students and stakeholders need to balance efficiency, safety margins and commercial trade-offs in a transparent, traceable way. 
The app computes, for user-created or templated voyages, all major voyage metrics: leg distances and days at sea (by laden/ballast speeds), allowances for adverse weather and safety margins, port days based on loading/discharge rates, different fuels' consumption, per-leg and aggregate fuel purchases and costs (with two user-supplied bunkering choices and automatic en-route top-ups), port and canal costs, voyage OPEX, gross freight, commissions and the resulting net profit and Time Charter Equivalent (TCE). 
Outputs include a compact scenario summary table, per-scenario detailed breakdowns presented per leg (distance, days, fuel volumes, and per-leg fuel costs), and downloadable cost visualizations (comparative for scenarios, and per leg contributions).
NeoCharter’s design deliberately emphasizes educational clarity: constants and assumptions are visible and editable, templated voyages illustrate typical real-world cases (coal, iron ore, grain) and a custom mode lets students construct continuous multi-leg trips using a global port list. The interface encourages exploration (e.g., change one parameter and immediately compare Net Profit and TCE) and explicitly communicatign assumptions about bunkering, fuel allocation, and other important results, so the users can critically assess their decisions. NeoCharter therefore serves as a compact, hands-on laboratory for maritime students and stakehlders to practice data-driven chartering decisions and understand the economics and operational constraints of modern bulk shipping.
